Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how the Tien Len app (Tien Len and Tien Len Communist) handles information. It applies to the app on iPhone and iPad.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no purchases.
  • Your scores and game progress are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following on your phone or tablet only:

  • your chosen language;
  • scores, games played and finishing places for each game mode;
  • the round in progress and the last screen you had open.

This information is never sent to us. You can delete it with the Reset buttons on the Scores screen, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob (banner and full-screen ads). To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system, app version and how you interact with ads. This information is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

In regions where it is required (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can also limit ad tracking at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone or iPad.

Update check

When the app starts, it checks whether a newer version is available so it can ask you to update. It asks Apple's App Store for the app's latest version. This check does not include any personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It contains no gambling or betting of any kind. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it. Ad-related data is handled by Google as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: yimasupport@gmail.com

本政策说明 Tien Len 应用（越南十三张·自由 和 越南十三张·共产）如何处理信息。本政策适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 版本。

概要

  • 我们不会索取您的姓名、电话号码、电子邮件、联系人、照片或位置。
  • 没有账号、无需登录，也没有任何付费购买。
  • 您的积分和游戏进度只保存在您的设备上。
  • 本应用展示 Google AdMob 广告。Google 可能会为展示和衡量广告而收集设备信息，详见下文。

保存在您设备上的信息

为了让您从上次离开的地方继续，本应用仅在您的手机或平板上保存以下信息：

  • 您选择的语言；
  • 每种玩法的积分、已玩局数和名次；
  • 正在进行的牌局以及您上次打开的页面。

这些信息从不发送给我们。您可以通过「积分」页面的「重置」按钮删除它们，或直接卸载本应用。

广告（Google AdMob）

本应用免费，由 Google AdMob 广告（横幅广告和全屏广告）提供支持。为了投放、限制和衡量广告并防止欺诈，Google 可能会收集和使用以下信息：设备的广告标识符、IP 地址、设备型号、操作系统、应用版本以及您与广告的互动情况。这些信息由 Google 按其自身政策处理：

  • Google 隐私权政策
  • Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息

在法律要求的地区（例如欧洲经济区和英国），本应用会在展示个性化广告前征求您的同意。您也可以随时在 iPhone 或 iPad 的 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 中限制广告跟踪。

版本检查

应用启动时会检查是否有新版本，以便提示您更新。这会向 Apple App Store 查询本应用的最新版本。此检查不包含任何个人信息。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，且不包含任何赌博或下注内容。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

安全与共享

我们不出售或共享个人信息，因为我们不收集个人信息。与广告相关的数据由 Google 按上文所述处理。

政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面及其生效日期。
